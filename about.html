A LITTLE BACKGROUND

My Story

I grew up around my family's Filipino restaurant in Arkansas, where I got to see firsthand what goes into building a business. From connecting with customers to finding creative ways to bring people through the door, that experience sparked my interest in business and marketing.

Over time, that curiosity led me into web development, technology, and finance. I've had the chance to work on everything from digital marketing for small businesses to product marketing at BlackRock in New York.

What I enjoy most is finding ways to make complicated ideas easier to understand, whether that's through a website, a marketing campaign, or research.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me teaching Pilates, traveling, taking photos, or finding somewhere new to eat.
