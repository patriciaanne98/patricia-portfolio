Project Overview

The Little Red River Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to protecting and preserving Arkansas's Little Red River through conservation, research, and community engagement.

As the project lead of Project Nurture, a four-person capstone team at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, I led a collaborative initiative to strengthen the foundation's digital presence through website development, CRM integration, and digital content strategy.

Our goal was to create a connected digital experience that improves access to river resources, communicates conservation initiatives, and strengthens relationships with donors, members, and the community.

My Contribution

As project lead, one of my priorities was understanding what the Little Red River Foundation wanted to accomplish and translating those goals into actionable steps for our team. With each team member taking ownership of their respective areas, I worked to keep everyone aligned while maintaining communication with the foundation's stakeholders.

Alongside my leadership responsibilities, I contributed directly to website design and development, information architecture, and content strategy. I also presented our progress to the client, incorporated their feedback, and developed resources to help them manage the website independently after our handoff.

Throughout the project, I learned that leadership is as much about listening and adapting as it is about making decisions. Balancing different perspectives, technical challenges, and client expectations taught me how to approach problems collaboratively while keeping the organization's larger goals in mind.
