01 / OVERVIEW

Digital Doesn't Mean Immaterial.

A search, a click, or a conversation with artificial intelligence may feel effortless. Behind these interactions, however, is a physical network of processors, data centers, cooling systems, and electrical infrastructure.

Through an independent study at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, I examined how Google Search and ChatGPT shape user behavior and how interface design may influence computing demand. The research connects digital experience design with the physical resources that make artificial intelligence possible.

02 / KEY INSIGHTS

Putting Digital Demand Into Perspective.

8% vs. 15%

Interfaces Shape Behavior

Pew Research Center found that users clicked traditional search results less frequently when an AI-generated summary appeared.

2.6B

Individual Interactions Add Up

ChatGPT received approximately 2.6 billion messages per day in June 2025, illustrating the scale at which individual digital interactions accumulate.

945 TWh

Digital Demand Is Physical

The International Energy Agency projects that global data center electricity consumption could reach approximately 945 terawatt-hours in 2030.

Sources: Pew Research Center, NBER research on ChatGPT usage, and the International Energy Agency. These figures describe separate observations and projections, not a measured causal relationship between interface design and electricity demand. Full references are included in the research paper.
